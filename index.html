About Me

Turning Roots into Strength

"My mission is to help you grow from your roots all the way to your roof — without apology, without compromise."

— Edith Esong Enowbi (E³)

I was born and raised in Cameroon, with parents who deeply believed in education and instilling self-confidence. I grew up with one goal — to study as much as possible, share my knowledge, and create as much impact as I could.

I moved to Germany in the year 2000, and the puzzle pieces of my dream of becoming an impactful self-confidence coach began falling into place.

Today, based in Berlin, I work with individuals and businesses across the globe — helping teams perform better, and helping people become the fullest version of themselves.
