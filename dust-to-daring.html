About the Book

A Revolution of Becoming, Not Repairing

Most self-help books tell you to fix yourself. Edith tells you to become yourself. There's a profound difference — and it changes everything.

Dust to Daring is a deeply personal and universally resonant guide that challenges the narrative that we are broken things in need of repair. Instead, it invites you into a quiet revolution — one that begins with unearthing your authentic voice and trusting the process of becoming.

Drawing from lived experience, Edith weaves together raw honesty, wisdom, and practical reflection to help you step out of survival mode and into the bold, unapologetic life you were designed to live.

Meet the Author

Edith Esong Enowbi

Known as E³, Edith Esong Enowbi is a voice that refuses to be silenced. A writer, speaker, and transformation guide, she has spent years walking through her own dust — and emerged daring.

Her work sits at the intersection of lived experience and purposeful living. She believes every person carries a voice worth hearing, and that the journey inward is the most courageous one we'll ever take.

Through Dust to Daring, Edith invites you to stop performing for the world and start becoming for yourself — one honest, brave step at a time.
